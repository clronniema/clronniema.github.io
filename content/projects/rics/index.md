Our team assembled 2 vans with SBG GNSS modules and a camera. Each van is driven in their respective region, collecting the latest status of road conditions.

This also includes an in-house developed integration system to streamline data collection process, GNSS post-processing and data transferring from the drivers. We were also responsible for editing data, when the quality is poor in such dense urban canopy.

My duties were mainly to develop the GIS portions, setting up the GNSS hardware, integrating data post-processing pipeline and overseeing the data editing process.
