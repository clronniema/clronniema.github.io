When planning a new development project, the view of the building is, without a doubt, valuable. Solar radiation is also an area that planners should consider. ArcGIS Pro offers the tools create maps of solar radiation received for a given area. The image above is an example of applying the potential solar radiation to the potential new building. 

{{< video src="shadows.mp4" controls="yes" >}}

Shadow impact is also crucial to decide how the building can affect the buildings nearby. One of the outputs could be a raster of areas that receive x hours more or less shadows. Another is a timelapse video illustrating how the shadows change over a day.

ArcGIS is sophisticated in terms of the solar related functions. Often, the functions allow users to specify date and time parameters precisely for very specific results.
