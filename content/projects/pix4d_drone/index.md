One of my duties were carry out ad hoc drone flights in order to form orthophotos or 3D models. These products are then used by internal engineers to facilitate communications.

While Pix4Dmapper is a descent product in simplifying the data collection process, harsh conditions could often cause defectious products. The Litchee app is an alternative that allows web-based pre-flight planning, customizable flight path and controlled image taking intervals. This approach is time consuming in the office, but ensures the quality of data collected on site.
