Prices of homes are often dependent on the quality of the view, therefore, the tool in ArcGIS is useful in assessing this criteria. The image above is an example of the interactive viewshed tool, where the green area would be visible and the purple area are obscured by buildings. The tool also offers flexibility in azimuth, tilt and angle of the viewshed.
