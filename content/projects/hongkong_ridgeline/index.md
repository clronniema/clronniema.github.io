Hong Kong is blessed with beautiful ridges on each side of the harbour, combined with many modernized buildings. There are regulations restricting buildings from blocking the view of the ridge on the observation points on the opposite side of the harbour.

{{< figure src="hk_ridgeline.PNG" title="Hypothetical new building obscures ridgeline" lightbox="true">}}


This exercise is a hypothetical scenario roughly based on the height restriction regulations. Through building the viewshed from the tools in ArcGIS Pro, it is then possible to intersect the derived viewshed and the potential new building. In this case the new building obscures the building free area of the ridge, violating the regulations.
